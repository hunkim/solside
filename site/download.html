Install

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Solside.app onto Applications.
  2. Solside is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized, so it opens normally. If macOS asks, choose Open.
  3. For AI features, open the ⚙ settings in the AI panel and paste your UPSTAGE_API_KEY.
